Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Ash Creative Clipper (contact: ashcreativeclipper@gmail.com) handles information in connection with its internal content publishing application ("the App").

Data We Access

The App uses Google OAuth with the following YouTube scopes for the operator's own authorized Google/YouTube account(s): youtube.upload, youtube.readonly, and yt-analytics.readonly. This allows the App to upload and schedule videos, read basic channel and video metadata, and read basic YouTube analytics for those channels.

How We Use Data

Accessed account information is used solely to operate the App: publishing and scheduling owned video content and reviewing its performance. OAuth tokens are stored only on the operator's own machine. We do not sell, rent, or share this information with third parties. This site does not use advertising cookies or third-party trackers.

Who Can Use the App

The App is an internal, personal-use application. It is not distributed to, and does not accept data from, general public users. Only the operator's own Google account is authorized.

Your Controls

You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time via Google Account > Security > Third-party app access, or by contacting us. For data deletion requests, contact ashcreativeclipper@gmail.com.

Changes

We may update this policy by posting changes on this page with a new "last updated" date.
